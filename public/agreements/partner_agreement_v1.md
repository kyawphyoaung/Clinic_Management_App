
# Referral Partner Agreement

## 1. Purpose
The Referral Partner agrees to refer prospective international patients seeking health screening or medical treatment in Taiwan to the Facilitator. The Facilitator will coordinate consultations, appointments, travel-related medical arrangements, and communication with partner clinics. This Agreement does not create an employment, agency, franchise, or joint venture relationship between the Parties.

## 2. Scope of Services
The Facilitator is responsible for:
- Patient coordination  
- Clinic appointment scheduling  
- Medical report coordination  
- Telemedicine arrangement (when applicable)  
- Treatment package quotation  
- Travel coordination support  
- Interpreter coordination (if applicable)  
- Post-treatment follow-up coordination  

The Referral Partner is responsible only for introducing potential patients and assisting with pre-referral communication.

## 3. Referral Process
The Referral Partner shall:
- Submit patient information using the official referral process.  
- Use only approved referral forms.  
- Obtain the patient's consent before sharing medical information.  
- Use official treatment quotations only.  

## 4. Commission
The Referral Partner is entitled to receive referral commission in accordance with the current Referral Partner Commission Policy.

Commission is payable only when:
- The patient completes treatment.  
- Full payment has been received by the partner clinic.  
- The referral is verified by the Facilitator.  

Commission percentages, payment schedules, and eligibility are governed by the separate Commission Policy, which forms part of this Agreement.

## 5. Pricing Policy
The Referral Partner shall not:
- Change treatment prices.  
- Offer unauthorized discounts.  
- Collect medical fees on behalf of the clinic unless expressly authorized in writing.  

Only official package prices issued by the Facilitator should be presented to patients.

## 6. Medical Disclaimer
The Referral Partner shall not:
- Diagnose diseases.  
- Recommend medical treatments.  
- Prescribe medications.  
- Guarantee treatment outcomes.  
- Provide medical opinions.  

## 7. Patient Privacy & Confidentiality
The Referral Partner agrees to keep confidential all patient information, prorated commission calculations for value-added referral services, business information, and marketing materials. Referral Partner will only share the patient ID code, and the patient shall share his or her medical notes with the attending physician directly.

## 8. Intellectual Property
All logos, trademarks, brochures, marketing materials, treatment information, photographs, and educational content remain the property of the Facilitator or the respective partner clinics. The Referral Partner is permitted to use only approved materials and solely for authorized promotional activities.

## 9. Non-Exclusivity
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, this Agreement is non-exclusive. Either Party may enter into similar agreements with other business partners for referral services.

## 10. Term and Termination
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice. The Facilitator may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Referral Partner:
- Provides misleading information  
- Violates pricing policies  
- Breaches patient confidentiality  

## 11. Limitation of Liability
The Facilitator is responsible only for coordination services.

The partner clinic and attending physician are solely responsible for medical consultation, diagnosis, treatment, clinical decisions, and treatment outcomes.

## 12. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan).

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be resolved through good faith negotiation. If unresolved, the Parties agree to mediation before commencing litigation. The Taiwan Taipei District Court shall have jurisdiction as the court of first instance.

## 13. Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with the Referral Partner Commission Policy and Partner Handbook, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions or understandings relating to its subject matter.
