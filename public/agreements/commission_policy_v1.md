# Commission Policy

**Version:** v1  
**Effective Date:** 2026

## Overview

This Commission Policy describes how referral partner commissions are calculated, reviewed, and paid for completed treatment cases referred to REVIVORA partner clinics.

## Commission Tiers

| Monthly Completed Cases | Commission Rate |
| ----------------------- | --------------- |
| 1–5                     | 5%              |
| 6–10                    | 6%              |
| 11–20                   | 7%              |
| 21+                     | 8%              |

## Payment Terms

- Commissions are calculated after treatment completion and deposit reconciliation.
- Payment schedules and methods are communicated by the Facilitator upon partner approval.
- Disputes must be raised within 30 days of the commission statement date.

## Adjustments

The Facilitator may adjust tier thresholds or rates with written notice. Continued referral activity after notice constitutes acceptance of updated terms.

---

*By agreeing to this policy, you acknowledge that commission rates are tier-based, subject to verification of completed cases, and may be updated in accordance with this document.*
