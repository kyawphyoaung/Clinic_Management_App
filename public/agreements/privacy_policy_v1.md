
# Privacy & Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose
This Privacy & Data Protection Policy explains how the Facilitator collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and retains personal information and medical information obtained from patients, referral partners, and website visitors. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

## 2. Scope
This Policy applies to:
- Patients  
- Prospective patients  
- Referral Partners  
- Website visitors  
- Business partners  
- Individuals who contact us regarding our services  

## 3. Information We Collect
Depending on the services requested, we may collect:

### Personal Information
- Full name  
- Date of birth  
- Gender  
- Nationality  
- Passport information  
- Contact number  
- Email address  
- Residential address  
- Emergency contact information  

### Medical Information
- Medical history  
- Current symptoms  
- Previous treatments  
- Laboratory reports  
- Imaging reports  
- Medication history  
- Physician referral letters  
- Medical questionnaires  

### Travel Information
- Intended travel dates  
- Flight information  
- Accommodation details (when required)  
- Visa-related information (if voluntarily provided)  

### Payment Information
- Booking & Coordination Deposit records  
- Payment confirmations  
- Transaction references  

We do not intentionally store complete credit or debit card information unless required by an authorized payment service provider.

## 4. How We Use Your Information
Your information may be used to:
- Arrange medical consultations  
- Coordinate telemedicine appointments  
- Schedule clinic appointments  
- Prepare medical quotations  
- Communicate with partner clinics  
- Coordinate travel-related medical services  
- Respond to inquiries  
- Process bookings  
- Process referral commissions (where applicable)  
- Improve our services  
- Comply with applicable legal obligations  

## 5. Medical Information
Medical information is collected only for the purpose of facilitating medical consultation and treatment. Medical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating physician and partner clinic. The Facilitator does not independently diagnose medical conditions or provide medical treatment.

## 6. Sharing of Information
We may share information only with:
- Authorized partner clinics  
- Treating physicians  
- Telemedicine providers  
- Medical interpreters  
- Travel coordination service providers  
- Payment service providers  
- Government authorities where required by law  

Information is shared only to the extent necessary for providing requested services. We do not sell personal information.

## 7. International Data Transfer
Because our services involve international medical travel, personal information may be transferred between countries for the purpose of coordinating healthcare services. Such transfers may include communication with healthcare providers, referral partners, or service providers located outside the patient's country of residence. Reasonable safeguards will be implemented to protect personal information during these transfers.

## 8. Data Security
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Examples include:
- Password-protected systems  
- Access controls  
- Secure file storage  
- Limited staff access  
- Secure communication methods where practicable  

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention
Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to:
- Provide requested services  
- Maintain business records  
- Resolve disputes  
- Comply with legal obligations  

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

## 10. Patient Rights
Subject to applicable law, individuals may request to:
- Access their personal information  
- Correct inaccurate information  
- Update personal information  
- Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted  
- Withdraw consent for future processing (where applicable)  

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or contractual obligations.

## 11. Referral Partner Responsibilities
Referral Partners must:
- Obtain patient consent before collecting or sharing information.  
- Use only approved communication channels.  
- Maintain confidentiality.  
- Protect patient information from unauthorized disclosure.  
- Comply with all applicable privacy laws and program policies.  

## 12. Cookies and Website Analytics
If our website uses cookies or analytics tools, such technologies may collect limited technical information, including:
- IP address  
- Browser type  
- Device information  
- Pages visited  
- Website usage statistics  

This information is used to improve website performance and user experience. Users may manage cookie preferences through their browser settings where available.

## 13. Third-Party Services
Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

## 14. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy & Data Protection Policy from time to time. The latest version will supersede all previous versions and will become effective upon publication or written notification.
