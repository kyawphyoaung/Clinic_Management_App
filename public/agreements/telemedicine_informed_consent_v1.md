
# Telemedicine Informed Consent Form

## 1. Purpose
This Telemedicine Informed Consent Form explains the nature of telemedicine services provided through Revivora and obtains your consent to participate in remote medical consultations with our partner healthcare providers. By signing this form, you acknowledge that you understand the benefits, limitations, and potential risks of telemedicine and agree to receive healthcare services through secure electronic communication.

## 2. What is Telemedicine?
Telemedicine allows you to consult with licensed healthcare professionals using secure communication technologies such as video conferencing, telephone, or other digital platforms without being physically present at the healthcare facility.

Telemedicine services may include:
- Initial medical consultation  
- Review of medical history and symptoms  
- Discussion of laboratory or imaging results  
- Preventive health consultation  
- Wellness consultation  
- Aesthetic consultation  
- Follow-up consultation  
- Treatment recommendations  
- Referral to appropriate specialists or healthcare facilities  

## 3. Role of Facilitator
Unless specifically stated otherwise, the facilitator does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Medical advice, diagnosis, and treatment recommendations are provided solely by licensed physicians or healthcare professionals from our partner healthcare providers.

Facilitator's role includes:
- Coordinating appointments  
- Facilitating communication  
- Assisting with medical record collection  
- Providing language support where applicable  
- Coordinating travel and healthcare logistics  
- Supporting continuity of care  

## 4. Benefits of Telemedicine
Potential benefits include:
- Convenient access to healthcare professionals  
- Reduced travel before deciding on treatment  
- Earlier medical assessment  
- Improved continuity of care  
- Easier communication with international healthcare providers  
- Better preparation before traveling for in-person treatment  

## 5. Limitations of Telemedicine
I understand that:
- Telemedicine has limitations compared with an in-person physical examination.  
- Certain medical conditions require physical examination, laboratory testing, or imaging before diagnosis can be confirmed.  
- My healthcare provider may recommend an in-person consultation if clinically necessary.  
- No guarantee is made regarding specific treatment outcomes.  

## 6. Risks
Possible risks include:
- Technical interruptions  
- Audio or video quality issues  
- Delays caused by internet connectivity  
- Incomplete clinical information due to the absence of physical examination  
- Data security risks despite reasonable safeguards  

## 7. Privacy and Confidentiality
Revivora and its partner healthcare providers are committed to protecting your personal and medical information.

Telemedicine consultations may be conducted using secure communication platforms.

Medical information will only be shared with authorized healthcare professionals involved in your care and as permitted by applicable law and your consent.

## 8. Recording
Telemedicine consultations will not be recorded unless:
- You provide explicit consent; or  
- Recording is required by applicable law or healthcare regulations.  

If recording is permitted, you will be informed before recording begins.

## 9. Emergencies
Telemedicine is not appropriate for medical emergencies.

If you experience:
- Chest pain  
- Difficulty breathing  
- Stroke symptoms  
- Severe bleeding  
- Loss of consciousness  
- Any other medical emergency  

Please contact your local emergency services immediately.

## 10. Voluntary Participation
Participation in telemedicine is voluntary. You may decline or withdraw your consent at any time before or during the consultation without affecting your future access to healthcare services.

## 11. Consent
By signing below, I confirm that:
- I have read and understood this Telemedicine Informed Consent Form.  
- I have had the opportunity to ask questions.  
- My questions have been answered to my satisfaction.  
- I understand the benefits and limitations of telemedicine.  
- I consent to receive telemedicine services from licensed healthcare providers coordinated through Revivora.  
- I authorize Revivora to facilitate communication and securely exchange relevant medical information with partner healthcare providers for the purpose of my care, in accordance with applicable privacy laws and my separate privacy consent where required.
