
# Booking & Refund Policy

## 1. Purpose
This Booking & Refund Policy explains the booking process, payment requirements, cancellation procedures, and refund conditions for patients using the Medical Tourism Coordination Services provided by the Facilitator.

## 2. Booking Process
A booking generally follows these steps:

1. Patient Inquiry  
2. Patient Registration  
3. Medical Record Submission  
4. Medical Case Review  
5. Treatment Quotation  
6. Acceptance of Quotation  
7. Payment of the Booking & Coordination Deposit  
8. Pre-treatment Telemedicine Consultation (if applicable)  
9. Appointment Confirmation  
10. Travel to Taiwan  
11. Treatment  
12. Post-treatment Follow-up  

The Facilitator reserves the right to modify the booking sequence where clinically or operationally necessary.

## 3. Booking & Coordination Deposit
To begin appointment coordination and reserve medical services, the Patient must pay the required Booking & Coordination Deposit.

The deposit covers coordination activities such as:
- Case management  
- Medical record review coordination  
- Appointment scheduling  
- Telemedicine scheduling  
- Communication with partner clinics  
- Treatment planning coordination  
- Travel-related medical coordination  

Unless otherwise stated in writing, the deposit will be credited toward the official treatment package price if treatment proceeds.

## 4. Non-Refundable Deposit
Once coordination services have commenced, the Booking & Coordination Deposit is nonrefundable.

This is because administrative work, scheduling, communication, and coordination services begin immediately after payment.

The Patient acknowledges and agrees to this condition before making payment.

## 5. Treatment Fees
The Booking & Coordination Deposit forms part of the total treatment package price. The remaining balance is payable according to the instructions provided by the Facilitator or the partner clinic. Additional medical services, investigations, medications, or procedures not included in the original quotation may incur additional charges.

## 6. Patient Cancellation
If the Patient cancels after paying the Booking & Coordination Deposit:
- The deposit will not be refunded.  
- Any unused treatment fees paid beyond the deposit will be handled according to the applicable partner clinic policy, if applicable.

## 7. Rescheduling
Patients who need to change their appointment should notify the Facilitator as early as possible. The Facilitator will make reasonable efforts to assist with rescheduling. Rescheduling is subject to:
- Physician availability  
- Clinic scheduling  
- Medical suitability  
- Operational feasibility  

Additional fees may apply if imposed by the partner clinic or third-party service providers.

## 8. Patient No-Show
If the Patient fails to attend a confirmed appointment without prior notice:
- the Booking & Coordination Deposit will be forfeited; and  
- any future appointment will require a new booking process, unless otherwise approved by the Facilitator.

## 9. Visa Refusal
If a visa application is refused, the Patient should notify the Facilitator immediately and provide supporting documentation where reasonably requested. The Booking & Coordination Deposit remains non-refundable because coordination services have already been performed. The Facilitator may, at its sole discretion, assist with rescheduling the appointment where feasible.

## 10. Medical Unsuitability
If, after further medical evaluation, the treating physician determines that the Patient is not medically suitable for the requested treatment:
- the Booking & Coordination Deposit remains non-refundable; and  
- any prepaid treatment fees beyond the deposit will be handled in accordance with the partner clinic's refund policy.  

Where appropriate, the Patient may receive an alternative treatment recommendation from the treating physician.

## 11. Cancellation by the Partner Clinic
If the partner clinic is unable to provide the scheduled treatment due to physician unavailability, operational issues, or other circumstances beyond the Patient's control:
- the Facilitator will make reasonable efforts to arrange an alternative appointment or an appropriate alternative clinic; or  
- where applicable, coordinate with the partner clinic regarding refunds of prepaid treatment fees.

## 12. Force Majeure
Neither the Facilitator nor the partner clinic shall be liable for delays, cancellations, or service interruptions resulting from events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to:
- Natural disasters  
- Pandemic outbreaks  
- Government restrictions  
- War or civil unrest  
- Airline cancellations  
- Airport closures  
- Immigration restrictions  
- Public health emergencies  

The Facilitator will use reasonable efforts to assist affected Patients in rescheduling services.

## 13. Refund Processing
Where a refund is approved:
- refunds will be processed using the original payment method whenever reasonably practicable;  
- processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, banking institution, or partner clinic; and  
- bank charges, payment gateway fees, foreign exchange losses, or transfer fees may be deducted where applicable.

## 14. Third-Party Services
Payments made directly to third-party providers, including airlines, hotels, insurance companies, transportation providers, or other independent service providers, are subject to their respective cancellation and refund policies. The Facilitator is not responsible for third-party refund decisions.

## 15. Changes to Treatment Plan
The Patient understands that the final treatment plan may differ from the preliminary quotation following consultation with the treating physician. Additional procedures, investigations, or medications may result in additional costs.

## 16. Acceptance
By paying the Booking & Coordination Deposit or using the Facilitator's services, the Patient confirms that they have:
- read this Booking & Refund Policy;  
- understood its contents; and  
- agreed to be bound by its terms.
