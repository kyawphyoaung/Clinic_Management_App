
# Data Transfer Consent Form

## 1. Purpose
This Consent Form authorizes the Medical Tourism Facilitator ("Facilitator") to collect, use, store, disclose, and transfer my personal information and medical information for the purpose of arranging medical consultation, treatment, travel coordination, and related healthcare services in Taiwan. I understand that the Facilitator acts solely as a medical tourism coordinator and is not my treating healthcare provider.

## 2. Information That May Be Collected
I understand that the Facilitator may collect information including, but not limited to:

### Personal Information
- Full name  
- Date of birth  
- Gender  
- Nationality  
- Passport information  
- Contact information  
- Residential address  
- Emergency contact  

### Medical Information
- Medical history  
- Current medical condition  
- Laboratory reports  
- Imaging reports  
- Current medications  
- Previous treatments  
- Surgical history  
- Physician referral letters  
- Other medical records voluntarily provided by me  

### Travel Information
- Intended travel dates  
- Flight information  
- Hotel information (if applicable)  
- Visa-related information (if voluntarily provided)  

## 3. Purpose of Collection
I understand that my information may be used for the following purposes:
- Medical case assessment  
- Arranging telemedicine consultations  
- Preparing treatment quotations  
- Scheduling clinic appointments  
- Coordinating medical treatment  
- Travel coordination related to medical services  
- Interpreter arrangements  
- Post-treatment follow-up  
- Responding to my inquiries  
- Meeting legal and regulatory requirements  

## 4. International Transfer of Information
I understand that my personal information and medical information may be transferred between countries for the purpose of arranging my medical care. My information may be shared with authorized organizations including:
- Partner clinics in Taiwan  
- Treating physicians  
- Telemedicine providers  
- Medical interpreters  
- Travel coordination providers  
- Diagnostic laboratories (when applicable)  
- Government authorities where legally required  

I understand that my information may be transferred electronically using secure communication methods where reasonably available.

## 5. Confidentiality
The Facilitator will take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect my personal and medical information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. I understand that while reasonable safeguards are implemented, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Voluntary Consent
I understand that providing my personal and medical information is voluntary. However, failure to provide necessary information may prevent the Facilitator from arranging consultations, obtaining treatment quotations, scheduling appointments, or coordinating my medical travel.

## 7. My Rights
Subject to applicable law, I understand that I may request to:
- Access my personal information  
- Correct inaccurate information  
- Update my information  
- Withdraw this consent for future processing where legally permitted  
- Request deletion of information where applicable  

I understand that withdrawal of consent will not affect processing that has already occurred prior to my request.

## 8. No Medical Guarantee
I understand that:
- The Facilitator does not diagnose medical conditions.  
- The Facilitator does not provide medical treatment.  
- All medical decisions are made solely by the treating physician.  
- No guarantee of treatment outcome has been made.  

## 9. Consent
By signing below, I voluntarily authorize the Facilitator to collect, use, store, disclose, and internationally transfer my personal information and medical information as described in this Consent Form for the purpose of coordinating my requested medical services. I confirm that the information I provide is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge.
