# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose
This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical and professional standards expected of all Authorized Referral Partners participating in the Medical Tourism Referral Program. All Referral Partners are expected to conduct business with integrity, professionalism, honesty, and respect for patients, partner clinics, and fellow partners.

## 2. Professional Conduct
Referral Partners shall:
- Act honestly, ethically, and professionally at all times.
- Treat every patient with dignity, courtesy, and respect.
- Communicate accurately and responsibly.
- Maintain the reputation of the Facilitator and partner clinics.
- Conduct business in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## 3. Patient First Principle
The patient's health, safety, privacy, and best interests must always take priority over financial gain. Referral Partners shall never pressure patients into receiving medical treatment. Patients must always be free to make their own healthcare decisions.

## 4. Medical Representation
Referral Partners must not:
- Diagnose diseases.
- Recommend specific medical treatments.
- Prescribe medications.
- Interpret laboratory or imaging results.
- Guarantee treatment outcomes.
- Promise cure rates or success rates.
- Advise patients to stop or change existing medical treatment.

All medical advice must come directly from the treating physician.

## 5. Honest Marketing
Referral Partners shall:
- Use only official marketing materials approved by the Facilitator.
- Present treatment information accurately.
- Clearly distinguish between medical facts and personal opinions.
- Avoid misleading, exaggerated, or deceptive advertising.

Referral Partners shall not:
- Make false claims.
- Misrepresent clinic facilities or physicians.
- Use fake testimonials or fabricated patient stories.
- Alter official treatment package prices.

## 6. Pricing Integrity
Referral Partners must use only the official package prices provided by the Facilitator. Referral Partners shall not:
- Increase prices.
- Reduce prices.
- Offer unauthorized discounts.
- Collect medical treatment fees unless specifically authorized.

All quotations must originate from the Facilitator.

## 7. Patient Privacy
Referral Partners shall:
- Obtain patient consent before collecting personal or medical information.
- Protect all confidential information.
- Share patient information only through approved secure communication channels.
- Use patient information solely for arranging medical services.

Referral Partners shall never sell, disclose, or misuse patient information.

## 8. Professional Communication
Referral Partners shall:
- Respond to patient inquiries promptly and respectfully.
- Avoid offensive, discriminatory, or abusive language.
- Maintain professional behavior on all communication platforms, including email, messaging applications, and social media.

## 9. Conflict of Interest
Referral Partners shall disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest that may influence their recommendations. Referral Partners shall not accept undisclosed financial incentives from third parties that may compromise patient interests.

## 10. Compliance with Policies
Referral Partners agree to comply with all applicable program documents, including:
- Referral Partner Agreement
- Commission Policy
- Referral Ownership Policy
- Partner Handbook
- Privacy Policy
- Booking & Refund Policy
- Brand & Marketing Guidelines

## 11. Reporting Concerns
Referral Partners are encouraged to report suspected fraud, unethical conduct, patient safety concerns, privacy breaches, or policy violations to the Facilitator as soon as reasonably possible. Reports made in good faith will be handled confidentially whenever practicable.

## 12. Prohibited Conduct
Referral Partners shall not:
- Submit false patient information.
- Register patients without consent.
- Claim another Referral Partner's patient.
- Manipulate commission records.
- Offer unauthorized financial incentives.
- Accept bribes or kickbacks.
- Engage in fraudulent or illegal activities.
- Damage the reputation of the Facilitator or partner clinics through false statements.
- Use the Facilitator's name, logo, or branding without authorization.

## 13. Social Media Conduct
When posting online, Referral Partners shall:
- Use accurate and responsible healthcare information.
- Respect patient confidentiality.
- Obtain written consent before using patient testimonials, photographs, or videos.
- Avoid posting misleading medical content.

Referral Partners shall not present themselves as employees or official spokespersons of the Facilitator unless authorized in writing.

## 14. Compliance and Enforcement
Failure to comply with this Code of Conduct may result in:
- Written warning;
- Temporary suspension of Referral Partner privileges;
- Cancellation of pending commissions where permitted under the applicable agreements;
- Termination of the Referral Partner Agreement; and/or
- Legal action where appropriate.

The Facilitator reserves the right to investigate any suspected violation before taking action.

## 15. Amendments
The Facilitator may update this Code of Conduct from time to time. Referral Partners will be notified of material changes before they become effective.
