
# Referral Ownership Policy

## 1. Purpose
This Referral Ownership Policy establishes clear rules for determining which Referral Partner is entitled to referral ownership and commission for a referred patient. The purpose of this policy is to ensure fairness, transparency, and consistency while preventing disputes between Referral Partners.

## 2. Definition of a Referral
A referral is considered valid only when a Referral Partner submits a prospective patient's information through the Facilitator's official referral system. Submission through unofficial channels (e.g., personal messaging, verbal communication, or social media comments) does not establish referral ownership.

## 3. Referral Registration
To register a referral, the Referral Partner must provide sufficient patient information, including:
- Patient's full name
- Contact information
- Country of residence
- Requested medical service
- Preferred travel period (if known)

Additional medical records may be requested by the Facilitator if necessary.

## 4. Referral Validity Period
A registered referral remains valid for 60 calendar days from the date of registration. During this period, the Referral Partner is expected to actively assist the patient in completing the booking process. If no Booking & Coordination Deposit is received within the validity period, the referral will automatically expire.

## 5. Confirmation of Referral Ownership
Referral ownership is officially confirmed only when both of the following conditions have been met:
- The patient has been successfully registered through the official referral system; and
- The required Booking & Coordination Deposit has been received by the Facilitator within the referral validity period.

Only after these conditions are met will the Referral Partner become eligible for commission, subject to the Commission Policy.

## 6. Expired Referrals
If a referral expires because the patient does not proceed with the booking within the validity period:
- The original Referral Partner no longer retains referral ownership.
- The patient may subsequently be referred by another Referral Partner.
- The new Referral Partner must complete a new referral registration and booking process.

## 7. Duplicate Referrals
If the same patient is referred by more than one Referral Partner, the Facilitator will determine referral ownership based on this policy.

Priority will generally be given to the Referral Partner who:
1. Submitted first a complete referral through the official system; and
2. Successfully guided the patient to complete the required Booking & Coordination Deposit within the referral validity period.

The Facilitator reserves the right to review all communication records and booking history before making a final determination.

The Facilitator's decision regarding referral ownership shall be final.

## 8. Change of Referral Partner
A patient may request to work with a different Referral Partner before paying the Booking & Coordination Deposit. Once the Booking & Coordination Deposit has been received, referral ownership will normally remain with the confirmed Referral Partner unless otherwise approved by the Facilitator under exceptional circumstances.

## 9. Commission Eligibility
Referral ownership alone does not guarantee commission.

Commission is payable only when all requirements set out in the Referral Partner Commission Policy have been fulfilled, including:
- Successful treatment completion;
- Full payment received by the partner clinic; and
- Compliance with all Referral Partner policies.

## 10. Fraudulent or Unethical Conduct
The following actions are strictly prohibited:
- Submitting false patient information.
- Registering patients without their knowledge or consent.
- Attempting to claim another Referral Partner's patient.
- Creating duplicate registrations.
- Manipulating referral records.
- Offering unauthorized discounts or incentives.
- Misrepresenting medical services or treatment outcomes.

Any violation may result in:
- Cancellation of referral ownership;
- Loss of commission;
- Suspension or termination of Referral Partner status; and
- Any other action deemed appropriate by the Facilitator.

## 11. Privacy and Confidentiality
Referral Partners must maintain the confidentiality of all patient information in accordance with the Referral Partner Agreement, Privacy Policy, and applicable data protection laws. Patient information may only be collected, used, and shared for the purpose of coordinating medical services.

## 12. Policy Interpretation
The Facilitator reserves the right to interpret and apply this policy in good faith to ensure fairness and protect the interests of patients, Referral Partners, partner clinics, and the Facilitator.

## 13. Policy Amendments
The Facilitator reserves the right to amend this Referral Ownership Policy at any time. Updated versions will become effective upon written notice to Referral Partners.
